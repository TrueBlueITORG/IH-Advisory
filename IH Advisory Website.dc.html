About IH Advisory

Trusted Advisors for Organisations Navigating Change

IH Advisory is a 100% Indigenous and Veteran owned business, founded and operated in the Northern Territory. Our partners bring more than 60 years of combined leadership and consulting experience across Commonwealth and Territory government, Defence, mining, healthcare and the community sector.

We focus on three things that determine whether an organisation thrives — strategy, culture and capability — and we stay through implementation, so advice becomes change that lasts.

Our Story

Over 60 years of combined experience, now working for you.

60+ years of leadership & consulting experience

IH Advisory is a 100% Veteran and Indigenous owned local Northern Territory business. We partner with you to deliver strategic business development and tailored solutions that drive your organisation forward.

Our foundational expertise is built on over 60 years of combined experience gained through direct employment and high-level consulting across Commonwealth and State government, not-for-profits, Defence, mining, construction, healthcare and tourism.

This cross-sector expertise gives us the versatility and depth of knowledge to solve complex challenges for any organisation. From targeted interventions to enterprise-wide transformations, we have the capability to analyse, optimise and resolve your unique challenges.

Indigenous & Veteran Ownership

Two traditions of service, one way of working.

Our Indigenous ownership grounds us in respect for culture, community and Country, and in the understanding that lasting change is built through relationships. Our Veteran ownership brings the discipline, accountability and calm judgement learned leading people in demanding environments.

Together, they shape how we work: listen first, tell the truth, and stay until the job is done.
